Overview

What It Does

AI Resume Lab applies machine learning and natural language processing to a problem most job seekers tackle manually: making sure a resume actually lands. The system ingests resumes and job descriptions, extracts meaningful signals from both, and uses ML models to surface optimization opportunities — closing the gap between what a candidate has written and what an ATS or hiring manager is actually looking for.

The project demonstrates practical applied AI: not theoretical pipelines, but a working system with real input, real processing, and actionable output. It's built on the same data manipulation and modeling stack used in enterprise analytics — pandas, NumPy, and the Python ML ecosystem — applied to a use case that directly impacts career outcomes.

How It Works

The Process

01
Data Collection
Ingest resume templates and job descriptions as the training and evaluation corpus — building a structured dataset that reflects real-world hiring patterns across roles and industries.
02
Feature Extraction
Apply NLP techniques to pull structured signals from unstructured text — skills, titles, action verbs, quantified achievements, and keyword density — from both the resume and target job descriptions.
03
Analysis
Run ML models against the extracted features to score the resume, identify pattern gaps, and surface optimization opportunities ranked by likely impact on ATS scoring and recruiter screening.
04
Generation
Use AI to suggest targeted improvements — rewording bullet points, surfacing missing keywords, recommending stronger action verbs, and proposing content additions aligned to the target role.
05
Testing & Validation
Validate suggestions against job market trend data — ensuring recommendations reflect what employers are actually looking for, not just keyword stuffing.
06
Iteration
Continuously improve models based on outcomes — closing the feedback loop to make the system smarter over time as more data flows through it.
